Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short: Sakka Mini keeps your squad and settings on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

1. Who we are

Sakka Mini (package com.x5njr.sakkamini) is an Android app developed by Habib AlMawali, Muscat, Oman. In this policy, “we” means the developer.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only in its private storage on your phone:

  • Team name, player names, shirt numbers and availability you enter.
  • Your lineup and chosen formation.
  • App preferences: theme, text size, haptic feedback and the last open tab.

This data never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app. Reset preferences in Settings restores the default preferences.

3. Data we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in or email address is required.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking services are used.

4. Sharing a lineup

When you tap the share button, the app creates an image of your lineup and passes it to the Android share sheet. You choose the app that receives it, such as WhatsApp. That app’s own privacy policy then applies. We do not receive the image.

5. Permissions

Sakka Mini uses the vibration capability for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings. It does not request any dangerous permissions.

6. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Because data stays on your device, its security depends on your device’s own protections, such as a screen lock and updates.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Habib AlMawali through habib.al-mawali.com.
